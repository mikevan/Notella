# Episode foundation v1

The first episode contract, selected graph/evidence validators, explicit rule baseline and provisional teaching fixtures are implemented. The final no-model tutorial passes and all 104 software tests pass. The receipt records source hashes, counts and reference trajectories.

There are seven teaching branches, 20 prefix states, 11 distinct normalized visible prefixes and one original authored family. They are related synthetic fixtures, not independent incidents or an evaluation benchmark. Frozen source JSON is in profiles/network_episodes. Every source has trainingAllowed=false; the tutorial verifies that compilation refuses training admission. No neural model was loaded, trained or evaluated in this milestone.

The reference paths demonstrate contextual -> high -> critical escalation, de-escalation under a scoped benign explanation, critical -> high after compromise-support retraction, no change from unrelated-asset evidence, no escalation from duplicate reports, correct availability boundaries for late arrivals, and review deferral for unresolved clocks. These are outputs of a proposed authored rule policy, not evidence of AI understanding or field correctness.

Tests exercise future references, false support counts, duplicate nodes, retracted support, unsupported temporal order, unresolved clock domains, cross-asset links, erased history, correction relations, shared-prefix split relabelling and refusal of unadmitted training. A separate test demonstrates that structural contract validity does not establish correct criticality.
