# Typed-input and output-contract diagnostic v5

This is an inference-only pipeline diagnostic using the same v4 adapter and a matched unadapted base. No training occurs. The typed input, trusted system instruction and stronger allowed-state output schema change together, so their individual effects are not isolated.

Raw captured text, free-form context and external review_policy records are retained in original artifacts but excluded from this decision task's model view. Context is supplied as a trusted boolean by the profile owner. Descriptive legacy fixture kinds and area labels map through closed dictionaries; unknown kinds/areas require review rather than guessing. This mapping is not a sensor classifier.

Source aliases resolve only through the explicitly authored synthetic registry. Distinct labels alone do not establish distinct origins. The model sees origin groups/counts and controlled relationship values, not raw source labels. The registry's reviewed flags are caller assertions, not independent verification or statistical independence.

Ten allowed output states are derived from the previously authored TRAINING states. The output checker validates combinations and necessary evidence/time relations but never rewrites an answer or supplies a replacement decision. Rejected outputs remain model failures. A passed check is not proof of ground truth.

Cohort: the prior ten primary cases and six robustness cases; three new prospective payload/alias probes; two unsupported-input specimens (unknown lineage and window-boundary uncertainty). The last two must be deferred, not counted as model passes. Input/contract hashes were frozen before this diagnostic generated outputs.

Report model decision agreement, contract passes, accepted-wrong and rejected-correct counts separately. Rejected or deferred cases do not increase accuracy. Preserve all generated text, hashes, projections and receipts. Compare base and adapter under identical typed prompts and greedy decoding. No field accuracy, independent acceptance, successful training repair or deployment qualification follows from this related synthetic cohort.
