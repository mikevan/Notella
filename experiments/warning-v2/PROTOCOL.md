# Frozen development comparison protocol

Prepared before reading the base/adapted outputs for this run. Two development-validation families only: corroborated local sequence and unresolved signal identity. These are not independent product acceptance cases.

Both conditions use the identical registered ontology and curriculum messages, native chat template with enable_thinking=false, no assistant target in inference context, greedy decoding, maximum 384 new tokens, same local base intake and NF4/FP16 runtime. Each rendered prompt is hashed and compared byte-for-byte across conditions. The adapted condition adds only the newly trained adapter.

Mechanical checks: strict JSON/schema validity; exact agreement for posture, combinedTrigger and causation fields. These do not establish explanation truth.

Interpretation checks, assessed separately against supplied observations:

1. Credible sequence: recognize that all supplied policy conditions are met, classify critical/met, and explain that urgency does not establish causation or a certain attack.
2. Unresolved signal: classify high/unconfirmed and explicitly identify uncertain drone identity as the missing condition.
3. Both: no fabricated observations, physical actor attribution, calibrated probabilities, or claimed attack outcome. No generic refusal may replace the policy comparison.

Report each exact output and hash. Preserve base, adapted and failed results. A tie means no demonstrated training advantage on these cases. Improvement on two inspected development cases cannot establish generalization; future evaluation needs prospectively frozen, independently reviewed material. Do not reuse previously inspected frozen-test answers as training targets.
