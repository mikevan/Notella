## Scope clarification

The owner clarified that this milestone is to get the LLM-based node-edge morphology approach working and test its behavior as evidence changes. Do not build a separate non-LLM reasoning architecture or start a broader formal research program. The rule baseline is an explicit comparator for the proposed policy, not a new competing AI implementation. Unadapted versus trained conditions measure practical adaptation behavior on the frozen scenarios; they do not isolate the causal value of morphology as a representation. Broader validation is deferred until requested.


Before model generation, scoring was clarified to report decision-field agreement independently from graph-schema validity. Contract-valid decision and full-state counts still require the stated validators; a correct label cannot rescue an invalid graph. Scorer/evaluator source hashes are captured with both conditions.
