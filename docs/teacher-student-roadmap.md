# Evolving toward a small semantic-morphology warning model

The ontology represents the semantic model. The trainer defines concepts, typed relationships and how evidence changes their support over time. The AI's intended capability is to interpret Semantic Ontology Morphology, rather than merely reproduce ontology names or a graph's visual form. This is a testable development direction, not a demonstrated reliable field capability.

Training and deployment have separate resource constraints. Train and assess a capable large teacher centrally; distill demonstrated skills into smaller students. Refugees receive finished local inference models and are not expected to train them. Current direct 4B comparisons do not establish this teacher/student architecture. Earlier Colibri drafting experiments also did not establish a reliable specialized teacher.

1. Diagnose the five hypotheses with fixed-weight and training-fit tests before another speculative curriculum rewrite.
2. Maintain dependable evidence identities, event/receipt times, provenance, duplicate roots and active/retracted state in the application. Supply the semantic state, changes and relevant incident history to the model. Do not insert target severity into those facts.
3. Establish a large teacher that reliably handles basic evidence, independent support, correction, scoped benign explanations, asset boundaries, uncertain order and realistic sequences. Review decisions and supporting citations, not just prose or JSON.
4. Distill reviewed capabilities into progressively smaller students. Evaluate which behaviors survive each reduction. Task-specific teacher/student transfer has research precedent, but our threat task remains unproven: [knowledge distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531), [Distilling Step-by-Step](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.507/).
5. Integrate broad ten-second monitoring advancing every second, meaningful semantic deltas and focused incident tracking. Preserve triggering evidence after capture expiry. A compact validated warning must not wait for optional lengthy explanation.
6. Test missed warnings, incorrect clearances, false alarms, evidence support, offline latency, memory, thermal and battery behavior. Feed reviewed failures back into centrally authored scenarios and versioned releases. Field observations update application state, not model weights automatically.

The immediate milestone is a teacher that reliably interprets a small, defined set of semantic changes. Then determine how small its student can become while retaining that capability. Model size alone, average agreement and finite tensors are insufficient acceptance criteria. Real provider integration, phone warning delivery and representative operational testing remain required.
